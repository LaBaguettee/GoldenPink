Eco represents the earthly logic of magic in this world. It is its own unique element containing an aspect that makes things whimsical and magically alive.

Essence is the manifestation of a person's unique Eco flowing through their soul. Those with a large pool of Eco gathered around their soul are prone to sensing it in their environment. Through understanding, they replicate this Eco from within to create magic.

Aura is the powerful manipulation of wild Eco present in the environment. It relies heavily on mental fortitude to pull energy around the user, granting immense enhancements to speed, strength, and durability.

Cado refers to a unique, natural mutation or specialty in a person's Essence, offering them abilities outside standard Eco manipulation.

Guilds are the powerful, established factions that dictate high-society politics, combat rules, and wealth distribution across the nation.

Vortex or Gravitational Anchor is the intense, crushing mental state Aura users must maintain to pull wild Eco into themselves. If focus breaks, the magic violently disperses.

Eco-weaving is the architectural art of embedding Eco into physical objects, creating self-sustaining magical technology like heated floors and floating chandeliers.

Great Evening represents a formal high-society matchmaking gala designed to secure political alliances and preserve ancient lineages through organized tournament combat known as the Dishes.

Old Eco describes the ancient, raw magical traits and mutations that wealthy aristocratic families desperately try to preserve through strict genetic bloodlines.
