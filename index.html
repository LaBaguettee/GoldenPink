Essence User (EU) is the manifestation of a person's unique Eco through their soul. People that have a big "pool of Eco" gathered around their soul, are more prone to be able to Sense Eco. Their senses are essentially able to feel the Eco in the air, in what they touch, what they eat, see, smell and even hear and compute that information within themselves. With the knowledge gathered, they are able to replicate the eco from within their soul and out, creating "Magic." If they can understand how something works, nothing is truly impossible to do.

Aura Users (AU) is the manipulation of Eco present in the environment. It relies heavily on mental fortitude. The more concentrated/in the flow state a person is, the more Eco gathers energy around the users allowing them to Move faster or punch harder or creating a protective layer on them or even straight up fly in some cases. Nobody is tied to being one or the other but using both at the same time is simply logically impossible. Using Aura to gather Eco will overload your inner Eco causing a disturbance of proper Eco Flow which in turn breaks concentration. In many cases depending on the individual, it is much easier to use one than the other.

Cado refers to a unique, natural mutation or specialty in a person's Essence, offering them abilities outside standard Eco manipulation.

Guilds are the powerful, established factions that dictate high-society politics, combat rules, and wealth distribution across the nation.

Vortex or Gravitational Anchor is the intense, crushing mental state Aura users must maintain to pull wild Eco into themselves. If focus breaks, the magic violently disperses.

Eco-weaving is the architectural art of embedding Eco into physical objects, creating self-sustaining magical technology like heated floors and floating chandeliers.

Great Evening represents a formal high-society matchmaking gala designed to secure political alliances and preserve ancient lineages through organized tournament combat known as the Dishes.

Old Eco describes the ancient, raw magical traits and mutations that wealthy aristocratic families desperately try to preserve through strict genetic bloodlines.
